# Detailed privacy policy draft (kept for future reference)

**Not the live copy.** The published `/privacy` page uses a short, plain-language version
(founder feedback, 2026-09-02: the fuller version reads too legal/scary for regular users).
This detailed draft covers the same facts with more precision and structure — keep it here in
case a future audit, a legal review, or a larger/more regulated client ever needs the fuller
version. Same underlying facts as docs/12 item 6; update both together if the data model changes.

---

## What we collect

- **Your account information** — email address, and any profile information you provide (such
  as your name).
- **Authentication events** — a record of when you sign in. This helps us keep accounts secure
  and understand whether the platform is being used.
- **Activity within your organizations** — for the organizations you belong to, we record what
  you did and when (for example, that you registered for an event, submitted an assignment, or
  booked an appointment). This helps administrators of your organization see participation and
  helps us operate the platform reliably.
- **Administrative access records** — if platform staff ever need to look at your account to
  help with a support request or investigate an issue, we keep a permanent record that this
  happened (who looked, when, whose account). You cannot be looked at without this being
  recorded.

## How long we keep it

Authentication and activity records are kept in detail for 90 days, then reduced to a summary
(for example, "last seen" and basic counts) that we keep permanently. The administrative access
record described above is kept permanently and cannot be shortened or deleted — see below for
why.

## Who can see it

Ordinary members of your organization cannot see this data about you. It is visible only to
your organization's administrators (for participation summaries) and to platform staff (for
security and support purposes).

## Deletion requests

You can ask us to delete your account and personal information at any time. When you do, we
will remove your profile information and disconnect it from your activity history.

**One deliberate exception:** the administrative access record described above (who looked at
an account, and when) is never deleted, even for accounts that are removed — it becomes
anonymous (no longer linked to your name), but the fact that a lookup happened, on that date, is
kept permanently. This is by design: a security record that can be erased on request isn't a
meaningful security record. It exists to protect you — so that any access to your account is
independently auditable, including after the fact.
