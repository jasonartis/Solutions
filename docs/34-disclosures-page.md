> addition, not explicitly accepted; OPEN = unanswered. Legal: [docs/31](31-questions-for-a-lawyer.md) B5–B7.

## 1. What the founder wants (FOUNDER)

His original wish: *"I also want to make sure the disclosure that names a module is only visible to users with that
module. So if someone does not have speed dating, they shouldn't see a disclosure about speed dating notes and have no
clue what that is about. Or we can have a more general statement saying some parts containing personal info is
disclosed as xyz."*

His proposal: *"A disclosures page with general as well as those of the modules the viewer of the page is part of, and
when they join a new module they see the disclosure that is getting added to their main disclosures page, like that?"*

So: **one page per person: a general section, plus one section for each module that person holds. Joining a module adds
that module's section (with a "new disclosure" notice).** A person who holds no module never sees a module's text.

## 2. Assistant additions (PROPOSED — not yet accepted)

1. Record which wording **version** each person saw, so after a change you can tell who must see it again.
2. Keep a module's section visible **after someone leaves the module**, for as long as the platform still holds their
   data from it.
3. Where a section has a choice (a checkbox, docs/33 §7), show the notice and the choice together.
4. The **general** statement stays on the public `/privacy` page with no module names; module detail is shown only to
   entitled people, at first use. A lawyer confirms the general text is specific enough (docs/31 B7).
5. Copy rules from the founder's standing feedback: no security mechanisms, no module-specific examples in public copy,
   no promises that may not hold.
