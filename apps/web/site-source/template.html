Questions

What people usually ask.

Do I need to know what software I need?

No. Figuring that out is my job, and sometimes the answer is that you don't need custom software at all.

Why not just use an off-the-shelf product?

Sometimes you should, and I'll tell you if so. But most businesses end up stitching several products together with spreadsheets and workarounds. Software built around how you actually work and how your data flows removes that.

Can't I build this myself with AI?

AI can produce something that looks right, but it often gets the details wrong: how data moves between systems, who can see what, and what breaks when the business changes. Getting those details right, protecting your information and keeping it working is the work. I take responsibility for all of it.

What if I want to stop?

You can stop after any stage. If you leave the managed service, you get a full export of your data. The exact terms are in your written agreement.

What if something happens to you?

Your agreement covers it. If I ever stop providing the service, you get at least 90 days' notice and a full export of your data. The exact terms are in your written agreement.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what we find. The first conversation is free, and each later stage has a fixed, written price before you commit to it.
